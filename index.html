FAQ

A few straight answers.

What it does — and what it never does.

Does Rate Guardian book or change my reservation?

Never. It watches prices and tells you. Cancelling and rebooking is always your action, on your accounts.

How accurate are the prices?

They’re real observed rates from public search, sampled daily — but hotels/OTAs show different prices by moment, room, and device. Treat our numbers as a strong signal, then verify at the link before you act.

What do I forward?

Any hotel booking confirmation text (email, app message). We read hotel, city, dates and price, then discard the message itself — we keep only those four facts.

What if rates drop but the hotel charges cancellation fees?

The rebook math shows gross savings only; check your rate’s cancellation terms before acting (we link them out; flexible rates are where this shines).

Free vs paid?

Free: one watch, alerts batched daily. SG$49/yr: instant alerts, ten watches, wedding mode, weekly savings report.

How do I pay?

Telegram Stars in-chat (2 taps). 14-day no-questions refund.

Which hotels/cities?

4,893 SG/SEA properties today; if yours is missing, /watch suggests the closest matches and coverage grows weekly.

Do you sell my data?

No. We store your Telegram id, your watch facts, and your savings. That’s the entire database of “you”.

Why a Telegram bot?

Because your rates belong where your group chats already are — no app to install, nothing to remember.

What it is NOT

Not a booking agent, not a chat concierge, not price-guarantee insurance. A watchful friend with a spreadsheet.

The fine print, in plain words

Terms of Service & Privacy

What Rate Guardian does

Watches publicly available hotel prices for the hotels and dates you choose, and messages you in Telegram when prices move. What it never does: book, change, cancel, or pay for anything on your behalf. You are solely responsible for every booking, cancellation, and rebooking decision and for checking the final price at the provider before acting.

Prices are observations, not offers

Rates shown are best-effort samples of publicly available prices, collected periodically. Providers change prices constantly and by context; the price you see at checkout may differ. Rate Guardian gives no price guarantee, makes no booking, and is not travel insurance.

Your data

We store your Telegram user ID, the hotel/city/dates/price facts of what you ask us to watch, your tier, and your savings ledger. Booking confirmations you forward are parsed and immediately discarded — we never store the message itself. We don’t sell or share your data, ever. Delete everything with /delete or by emailing the operator.

Service changes
